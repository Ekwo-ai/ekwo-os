# Translations of the Kosovo pack

The pack's own files are written in Albanian, `defaults.language: "sq"` — the language every source this pack cites (Ligji Nr. 05/L-037 për TVSH-në, Ligji Nr. 06/L-032 për Kontabilitet) is published in officially, alongside Serbian.

- `en.json` — English, translated from the Albanian labels of `accounts.csv`, `taxes.json`, `tax_report.json`, `statements.json` and the legal mention of `pack.json`, by whoever wrote this pack. Not an official translation of any Albanian text: the accounts, tax codes and box names are Ekwo's own transcription (no official chart of accounts exists for Kosovo — see the pack's README), and this file is a second reading of that transcription rather than of the law itself.
