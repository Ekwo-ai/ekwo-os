# Where the wording of each language comes from

The pack's own files are written in English, because that is where its
reference chart of accounts and its statement line names come from: Canada
prescribes no chart of accounts in either official language, and the two
statement schemes are the presentation Sections 1520 and 1521 of Part II of
the CPA Canada Handbook — Accounting ask for, a text published in English and
in French but not free to quote.

## `fr.json` — French, as a Québec set of books keeps it

- **The taxes and the boxes of the return** are the wording of the French
  versions of the two administrations' own documents: the *Déclaration de la
  TPS/TVH — copie de travail* of the Canada Revenue Agency for lines 101 to
  115, 205 and 405, and the vocabulary of Revenu Québec — *TPS*, *TVQ*,
  *crédit de taxe sur les intrants*, *remboursement de la taxe sur les
  intrants* — for the taxes.
- **The provinces** are the French forms the Translation Bureau publishes and
  the ones the federal administration uses: *Colombie-Britannique*,
  *Nouvelle-Écosse*, *Île-du-Prince-Édouard*, *Terre-Neuve-et-Labrador*,
  *Territoires du Nord-Ouest*.
- **The accounts and the statement lines** are ordinary Québec bookkeeping
  French — *comptes clients*, *comptes fournisseurs*, *bénéfices non
  répartis*, *écart d'acquisition*, *frais payés d'avance*. They are a
  translation of this pack's own chart and not a transcription of a
  prescribed one, because there is none to transcribe.

English is the pack's own language and therefore carries no file: a declared
language that is the pack's own is refused.
