# Translations of the Rwanda pack

The pack's own files are written in English, `defaults.language: "en"`.

- `fr.json` — French. Rwanda's Value Added Tax Law, its law governing
  companies and its law on tax procedures are gazetted trilingually, in
  Kinyarwanda, English and French, each an official text of Parliament and
  not a translation of the others (see the three-column layout of the
  Official Gazette, cited throughout `pack.json`). Every label of a tax, a
  box of the Monthly VAT Declaration Form and a statement line that names a
  concept the French column of those gazettes also names is drawn from that
  column; the chart of accounts, which is original to this pack and which
  no Rwandan gazette transcribes in any language, is translated into the
  accounting French the OHADA-tradition packs of this repository already
  use (Sénégal, Côte d'Ivoire, Cameroun and the rest), so that a term reads
  the same across a francophone company's books whichever of them it holds.
  Not an official translation of the pack as a whole: the accounts, tax
  codes and box names are Ekwo's own transcription, and this file is a
  second reading of that transcription and, where the gazette's own French
  wording exists, of the gazette itself.

No Kinyarwanda translation is carried. Kinyarwanda is an official language
of Rwanda and the gazette's own Kinyarwanda column exists, but the
accounting vocabulary it would take to translate an account of a chart that
column does not itself carry has not been checked against how a Rwandan
bookkeeper actually speaks of a ledger — unlike a term of the Value Added
Tax Law, which the gazette states outright. Rather than invent that
vocabulary, this pack declares `en` and `fr` alone.
