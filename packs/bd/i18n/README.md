# Where the wording comes from

The pack is written in English (`defaults.language`): the Value Added Tax and
Supplementary Duty Act, 2012 has an Authentic English Text published by the
National Board of Revenue (S.R.O. No. 440-Law/2025/326-Mushak), and the Mushak
forms and the online return are published in English and in Bengali.

## `bn.json`

A **working translation of every label** into Bengali (`bn`): the country,
the chart, the journals, every tax code, every box of the return, every line of
the two statements and every account. It is the
pack's own translation, not an official Bengali version of any of the texts,
and the wording of the notes has not been compared with the Bengali Mushak 9.1
that the portal serves.

Bengali is listed in `languages` in `pack.json`, so the file covers every key
and `ekwo pack check` refuses a missing one. Covering every account of the chart
is work a Bengali-speaking accountant should read and correct.
