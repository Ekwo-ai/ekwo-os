# Languages of the China pack

`defaults.language` is `zh`, here meaning Simplified Chinese: the language of
the VAT Law, of its Implementing Regulations, of the return, of the accounts
appendix of the Application Guidance and of the Ministry of Finance's
statement formats. `languages` declares `en`, which `ekwo pack check` holds to
covering every account, journal, tax, box and statement line of the pack.

**No English label here is official.** Neither the Ministry of Finance nor the
State Taxation Administration publishes an English version of the accounts
appendix (财会〔2006〕18号), of 增值税及附加税费申报表, or of 会企01表 and
会企02表. Every English label is this pack's own translation of the Chinese
one, chosen to be recognisable to an English-speaking reader of Chinese
accounts — "surplus reserve" for 盈余公积, "paid-in capital" for 实收资本 —
and the Chinese is what binds. A reviewer who knows a translation used by the
Ministry of Finance in an official English publication is welcome to replace
this one with it and to cite it here.

The language code `zh` is also the one `packs/tw/` uses for Traditional
Chinese. The pack format carries a two-letter language and no script, so the
two cannot be told apart by their code; each pack's own labels are in its own
script.
