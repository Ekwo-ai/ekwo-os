# Languages of the Macao pack

`defaults.language` is `zh` (Traditional Chinese). `languages` declares `pt`,
the other official language of the territory, and `en`; `ekwo pack check` holds
both to covering every account, journal, tax, statement line and the chart's
name (the pack has no declaration boxes and no legal mention).

**Portuguese.** Statement lines transcribe the Portuguese text of the
Professional Accountants Committee's model financial statements (the *Guia
para a Aplicação das Normas Sucintas de Relato Financeiro*). Account names are
this pack's own wording in the vocabulary of those standards; Macao
publishes no chart whose names could be transcribed.

**English.** Not official. Macao publishes its legislation in Chinese and
Portuguese, and the standards' statements carry no English column. Every
English label is a translation for a reader, never for a filing; where the
Financial Services Bureau uses an English term (*Profits Tax*), the pack says
"complementary tax" and relies on the Portuguese name for precision.

The Chinese wording in the pack's own files is the one the accounts are kept
in; the Chinese names of the statements and lines follow common usage in Macao
(資產負債表, 損益表) and were not checked against the Chinese text of the
standards.
