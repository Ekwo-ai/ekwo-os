# Languages of the Moldova pack

`defaults.language` is `ro`: the language of the Codul fiscal, of the
Standardele Naționale de Contabilitate, of the Planul general de conturi
contabile and of forma TVA12. `languages` declares `en`, which `ekwo pack
check` holds to covering every account, journal, tax, box and statement line
of the pack.

**No English label here is official.** Neither the Ministry of Finance nor
the State Tax Service publishes an English version of Ordinul nr. 119/2013's
account nomenclature, of forma TVA12, or of S.N.C. 5's Forma nr. 1 and Forma
nr. 2. Every English label is this pack's own translation of the Romanian
one, chosen to be recognisable to an English-speaking reader of Moldovan
accounts — matching, where the underlying account is the same one, the
wording `packs/ro/` already uses for its own translation of a near-identical
chart. A reviewer who knows a wording the Ministry of Finance itself has used
in an official English publication is welcome to replace this one with it and
to cite it here.
